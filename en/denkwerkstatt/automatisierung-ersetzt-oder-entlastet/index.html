When automation replaces people – or relieves them of work

Automation does not have to mean excluding people from work.

March 11, 2026Technology, work and social development

01Main idea

Technology should take over repetitive, standardised and time-consuming tasks and thereby relieve human work.

AI should replace tasks – not necessarily people.

02Analysis

The idea builds on a conceptual work on a society of participation. One part of it is the further development and transfer of existing competencies.
