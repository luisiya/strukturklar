Projects

Specific questions from finance, public administration, processes and resources – structured according to a consistent approach.

How projects are labelled

Conceptual analysis:
independent examination and development of an approach.
Scenario analysis:
calculation or comparison of hypothetical options under explicitly stated assumptions.
Practical coordination:
practical work actually carried out, such as organisational coordination.
Implemented project:
only where implementation has actually taken place.

Hypothetical calculations should be read as scenarios based on assumptions, not as reliable forecasts or proven effects.

How I work in practice

In my practical work I analyse processes systematically, identify causes of errors and risks, structure work steps and develop my own checklists that support quality and reliability.
